I have a confession to make. I sort of retconned this _first_ blog post after writing the two following it.

I went back and forth on how best to write an "introduction" to this blog. I never could decide on a cool enough opening, so I skipped it for a bit and started writing about the shit I actually wanted to write about.

Starting something is hard. I struggle with fantasizing about the end result, or maybe interesting details in the middle. Finding the motivation to define a starting point that weaves the middle and end together is probably the hardest part for me. Already spent 100 words without saying anything.

## Real Intro

My name is Ray Cox. My hobbies come straight from the Midwest Techie starter kit: camping, kayaking, fishing, disc golf, beer (brewing & drinking!), 3d printing, vidya games, etc.

I've been a Software Engineer long enough I catch myself saying "Back in the day..." while discussing convenience solutions with my co-workers. Not quite long enough to retire as a goat farmer, although that is sounding __really__ good lately.

What is going on in the tech space anymore? No one can talk about anything other than fuckin' AI anymore.

### Lasciate ogne speranza, voi ch’intrate

If you haven't guessed by now this is not going to be a straight-edge work of literature. This is the one and only disclaimer on swearing I'll ever give.

## Rules of Engagement

I like to bridge and bastardize metaphors/phrases to fit in places where they don't really belong.

Anyways, here's some promises I'm making to myself and anyone who ever reads this nonsense.

### What this is _NOT_

This blog will never be about:
- AI
- Politics
- Pseudo-Intellectualism
- Ads/Shilling

I will also __never__ use Artificial Intelligence to write, revise, publish, or anything related to the creative writing process. We only do raw and unfiltered human stupidity here baby.

### What this... is?

I don't even know if I'm being honest. I just feel like I got shit to say, and it's kinda therapeutic to get it down on ~~markdown~~ paper. 

I probably should have just bought a journal or booked a therapist, but then I wouldn't have had an excuse to spend hours :sparkles: _building software_ :sparkles:.

I told you starting something was hard. *Start a blog* they said, *it will be fun* they said. I spent way too many hours scaffolding this blog together as a distraction while procrastinating the writing part.

Anyways, enjoy.
